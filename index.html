How this score works

Each listing gets a value score from 0 to 100, built from three weighted components:

  • 60% Affordability — how far the total price (item + shipping) is below your budget: 100 × (1 − total ÷ budget). A listing at your full budget scores 0; cheaper listings score higher.
  • 25% Condition — based on eBay’s condition label: New = 100, Refurbished = 80, Used / pre-owned or anything else = 65.
  • 15% Shipping affordability — how far shipping is below your shipping limit: 100 × (1 − shipping ÷ shipping limit). If your limit is $0, free shipping scores 100.

Things to keep in mind

  • Condition is approximate. It relies on the seller-chosen eBay condition label, not an inspection of the item.
  • Only listings that state a shipping cost, fit within your total budget, and stay within your shipping limit are included. Listings without a stated shipping cost are left out, even if they might be a good deal.
  • “Below / above median” compares each listing’s total price with the median total of all eligible listings found in this search.
  • Data comes from the eBay Browse API. Prices, shipping, and availability can change at any time, so check the listing on eBay before buying.
